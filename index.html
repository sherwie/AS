FAQ

Questions we hear every week.

Straight answers. No fluff.

How fast can you actually deploy?

5 days is our standard deployment timeline. Day 1 is your free audit. Days 2 to 4 we build and test. Day 5 you go live. No IT team required. No developer skills needed. We handle everything from configuration to CRM integration.

What does the AI agent actually sound like?

It sounds like your business. We build the agent voice around your brand tone, service offerings, and business rules. Clients regularly report that customers do not realize they are talking to an AI until after the appointment is already on the calendar.

What CRMs and tools do you integrate with?

We integrate natively with HubSpot, Salesforce, Go High Level, Jobber, ServiceTitan, Calendly, and most major calendar and CRM platforms. If your tool has an API, we can connect to it. Our internal stack includes Clay, n8n, Make, and Supabase for custom workflows.

What is included in the guarantee?

We measure your baseline before we start: missed calls, response time, and conversion rate. After 90 days we track the lift. If you do not see measurable improvement, we refund 50% of your first three months. No questions asked. We build the guarantee in because we know the system works.

Is my customer data secure?

Yes. We run on SOC 2 Type II compliant infrastructure with end-to-end encryption. We never share or sell customer data. Healthcare clients run HIPAA-ready configurations. All data stays in your designated environment.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No long-term contracts. Month to month after the initial setup. We have never had a client leave because the agent stopped working. Most clients expand to additional workflows within 90 days.

How does pricing scale as we grow?

Retainer scales with the number of workflows and channel coverage, not with conversation volume. You can 10x your lead volume without a change in monthly fee. When you outgrow a tier we upgrade you, and we credit any unused portion of the current month.
